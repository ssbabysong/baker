隐私政策

English · 技术支持 Support

面包物语（Bakery Days）· 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日（1.1 版起加入 iCloud 同步）

一句话：面包物语不收集你的任何个人数据。没有账号，没有统计、广告或追踪。你的配方、出炉记录和照片保存在你自己的手机上；打开 iCloud 同步时，也存一份在你自己的 iCloud 里，开发者看不到。

数据存在哪里

  • 配方、出炉记录、评分、笔记、照片、店名和设置，都保存在设备本地，不会上传到开发者的服务器。
  • iCloud 同步（iOS App，默认开启，可在「设置」里关闭）：配方、出炉记录、照片和店名会存进你自己 Apple 账号的 iCloud 私有空间，在登录同一个账号的设备之间同步。这部分数据由 Apple 按 Apple 隐私政策保管，开发者无法访问。关闭同步不会删除 iCloud 里已有的数据；要删除，可以在 iPhone 的「设置 → Apple 账号 → iCloud → 管理储存空间」里删掉本 App 的数据。
  • 删除 App 会一并删除这些数据。你可以在「设置」里随时导出备份文件，文件由你自己保管。
  • 桌面小组件显示的内容由 App 写在本机的共享存储里，同样不离开你的设备。

权限用在哪里

  • 相机、相册：只在你给出炉记录拍照、选照片或保存照片时使用。照片抠图（去背景）用的是系统自带的识别功能，在手机上完成，不上传。
  • 通知：只用来在发酵、烘烤计时结束时提醒你，是本地通知。

联网

除了上面的 iCloud 同步，App 只在以下两种情况联网，都不会发送你的个人数据或内容：

  • 从 GitHub Pages（ssbabysong.github.io）下载最新的内置配方列表。
  • 从 Google Fonts 加载显示数字用的字体。

和访问任何网站一样，这些服务商会收到你的 IP 地址等基础网络信息，由它们各自的隐私政策约束（GitHub、Google）。

AI 助手

导入配方页里的「让你的 AI 助手先整理一下」只是帮你复制一段提示词。App 本身不调用任何 AI 服务；你把内容发给哪个 AI 应用，由那个应用的隐私政策约束。

儿童

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

Privacy Policy

Bakery Days · Effective October 22, 2026

In short: Bakery Days does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Your recipes, bakes and photos stay on your own phone, and, with iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud, where the developer can't see them.

Where your data lives

  • Recipes, bake records, ratings, notes, photos, your shop name and settings are stored on your device and are never uploaded to the developer's servers.
  • iCloud sync (iOS app, on by default, can be turned off in Settings): recipes, bakes, photos and your shop name are stored in the private iCloud space of your own Apple Account and synced across devices signed in to it. Apple keeps this data under the Apple Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it. Turning sync off doesn't delete what is already in iCloud; to delete it, go to Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage on your iPhone.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. You can export a backup file from Settings at any time; the file stays with you.
  • Home-screen widgets read what the app saves in on-device shared storage. That data also never leaves your device.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you take, pick or save a photo of a bake. Background removal uses Apple's built-in on-device recognition; nothing is uploaded.
  • Notifications: used only for local alerts when a proofing or baking timer ends.

Network

Apart from iCloud sync, the app connects to the internet only to:

  • download the latest built-in recipe list from GitHub Pages (ssbabysong.github.io), and
  • load the font used for numbers from Google Fonts.

Neither request includes your personal data or content. As with visiting any website, these providers receive basic network information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies (GitHub, Google).

AI assistant

The "Let your AI assistant tidy it first" section on the import screen only copies a text prompt for you. The app does not call any AI service; whichever AI app you paste it into is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
