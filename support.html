常见问题

计时到了没有提醒？

到「设置 → 通知 → 面包物语」确认允许通知，并打开声音。专注模式或勿扰模式下，通知可能会被静音。

怎么添加桌面小组件？

长按主屏幕空白处 → 左上角「+」→ 搜索「面包物语」。有三种：我的店面（小）、配方架（中 / 大）、烘焙日历（中 / 大）。小组件如果暂时显示空白，打开一次 App 就会刷新。

换手机或删掉 App，数据会丢吗？

所有数据只存在这台手机上，删除 App 会一并删除。换手机前，请在 App 的「设置」里点「导出备份」，到新手机上用「从备份恢复」导入。

粘贴的配方没认出来？

导入页里有一段提示词，复制给你常用的 AI 助手，让它先把配方整理成标准格式，再粘贴回来。导入后也可以手动修改。

怎么切换语言和外观？

在 App 的「设置」里可以切换中文 / English，以及白纸 / 黑板两种外观。

Bakery Days · Support

A hand-drawn baking recipe app: shelve your recipes, time every proof and bake, and watch your little shop grow.

Having trouble or have an idea? Please open an issue on GitHub Issues with your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem.

FAQ

The timer ended but I got no alert

Go to Settings → Notifications → Bakery Days and make sure notifications and sounds are on. Focus or Do Not Disturb may silence them.

How do I add a widget?

Long-press an empty area of the home screen, tap "+" and search for "Bakery Days". There are three: My shop (small), Recipe shelf (medium/large) and Bake calendar (medium/large). If a widget looks empty, open the app once to refresh it.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app or switch phones?

Everything is stored only on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it. Before switching phones, tap Export under Backup in the app's Settings, then use Restore on the new phone.

My pasted recipe wasn't recognized

The import screen has a ready-made prompt: copy it into your favorite AI assistant to tidy the recipe into the right format, then paste the result back. You can also edit anything after importing.

How do I change the language or theme?

Open the app's Settings to switch between Chinese and English, and between the paper and chalkboard themes.
